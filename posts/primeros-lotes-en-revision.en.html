When an institution starts with Janium Collect, its first records do not go into the catalog on their own. Collect prepares the batches in full, just as it will prepare them later, but sets them aside: the Janium team loads them, the institution reviews the result in its catalog and, once it gives its sign-off, the following batches move on to automatic loading. Nothing reaches the production catalog without someone having seen it first.

This way, the launch is done with real records from the institution’s collection, and the cataloging decisions (which fields the catalog expects, which subjects it uses, how it wants names to appear) are adjusted on those records before the volume grows.

What each batch contains

A review batch is identical to an automatic-load batch, and it includes:

  • The records, in the file the catalog loads, in the institution’s format.
  • The quality report, with one row per record: its score, the problems detected field by field and the warnings about possible duplicates.
  • The cover thumbnails for each record, so that the catalog card shows the book’s cover.
  • The document for each record, if the institution asks for it: the full PDF or only the book’s table of contents, linked in the catalog next to the cover thumbnail and with the access level the institution decides.

The report is the tool for the review. Instead of opening the records one by one, the reviewer starts with the lowest-scoring records and the warnings, and sees at a glance whether a problem repeats across the whole batch or belongs to a single document.

From sign-off to automatic loading

The move to automatic loading is decided together with the institution and applies to the whole institution. Batches that have already been reviewed and loaded are not sent again: from that point on, only new records go directly to the catalog, with the same quality report in each batch to follow the work. If a record that is already in the catalog is sent again later, its cover thumbnail and its document are updated.

What was adjusted during the review is not lost in the change: the configuration that resulted from the first batches is the one the following batches use.

Batches that close with the capture

When the material arrives with separator sheets from the input channels, each run of books is a batch with its own name. The cover thumbnails arrive as the books are cataloged, and the records and the report are delivered together, under the batch name, when all of its books have been cataloged. If one falls behind, the batch is delivered anyway after a period without progress, and the report says which book was missing.

Limits

  • Per institution. The prior review applies to the whole institution, not to a specific collection.
  • Records already loaded. When a record that is already in the catalog is sent again, its cover thumbnail and its document are updated; changes to the catalog card are made in the catalog.
